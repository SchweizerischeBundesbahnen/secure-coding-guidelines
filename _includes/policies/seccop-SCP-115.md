{% if include.tier and include.tier == true %}<!-- markdownlint-disable MD041 -->![SHOULD](https://img.shields.io/badge/SHOULD-ffcc00.svg) {% endif %} **SCP-115: Perform threat modeling**

Conduct regular threat modeling sessions for critical authentication, access control, business logic, and key application flows.
Threat modeling helps identify potential security threats and vulnerabilities early in the development process.

Use structured approaches to systematically analyze potential attack vectors, assess risks, and design appropriate security controls.
Schedule threat modeling sessions for new features and major system changes to proactively address security concerns.
Threat modeling sessions should include security experts, developers, and relevant stakeholders to ensure comprehensive coverage of potential threats.
