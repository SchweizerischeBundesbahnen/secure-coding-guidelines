{% if include.tier and include.tier == true %}<!-- markdownlint-disable MD041 -->![SHOULD](https://img.shields.io/badge/SHOULD-ffcc00.svg) {% endif %} **SCP-121: Separation of responsibilities**

Separation of responsibilities refers to the principle that no user should be given enough privileges to abuse the system alone.
This principle helps to prevent fraud, errors, and misuse of the system by distributing power and accountability.
For example, the person who approves a paycheck should not additionally be the one who can issue it.

This principle can be implemented by dividing tasks and privileges among multiple users or roles, ensuring that critical functions require collaboration and oversight.
