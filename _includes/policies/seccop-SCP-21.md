{% if include.tier and include.tier == true %}<!-- markdownlint-disable MD041 -->![MUST](https://img.shields.io/badge/MUST-ff0000.svg) {% endif %} **SCP-21: Perform assets management of your software**

Proper inventory, ownership and lifecycle management of software components ensures that outdated or unmanaged components are identified and retired.
To address this:

- Maintain an up-to-date inventory of your software and components.

- Define and enforce deprecation policies to retire obsolete software and components promptly.

- When possible, create processes or automate the documentation of used software and components.
