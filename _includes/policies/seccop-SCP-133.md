{% if include.tier and include.tier == true %}<!-- markdownlint-disable MD041 -->![SHOULD](https://img.shields.io/badge/SHOULD-ffcc00.svg) {% endif %} **SCP-133: Verify integrity and authenticity**

When obtaining resources, ensure that their integrity and authenticity are verified.
This can be done by using checksums, digital signatures, or other mechanisms to ensure that the resource has not been tampered with and is from a trusted source.
