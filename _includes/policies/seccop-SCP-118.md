{% if include.tier and include.tier == true %}<!-- markdownlint-disable MD041 -->![SHOULD](https://img.shields.io/badge/SHOULD-ffcc00.svg) {% endif %} **SCP-118: Regularly check your vulnerability report**

Regularly review the reports produced by your vulnerability management tooling and take appropriate action to maintain or improve your product's security posture.
Such a platform provides centralized vulnerability management and monitoring.

Monitor it for security findings and remediation recommendations.
Address identified vulnerabilities according to their severity levels to keep your systems in a secure state.

Define and document how frequently these reviews have to happen.
