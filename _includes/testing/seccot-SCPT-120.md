To test this principle, the software should be subjected to various deployment scenarios, such as blue-green deployments, canary releases, and rolling updates.
Testcases should verify that the deployment process is automated and reproducible, and that it adheres to the security configurations and assumptions documented during development.
Furthermore, testcases should verify that the deployment environment is properly secured and that any sensitive information is protected.
