<!-- markdownlint-disable MD041 -->{% if include.header and include.header == true %}**SCPT-04: Testing - classify your data**{% endif %}

This policy cannot be automatically tested by tools.
During a review process, make sure to verify the following aspects:

- Data that is collected, stored, processed, or transferred, is properly classified.
- If data classification labels are used, verify that they are correctly applied.
- Make sure that personal data is handled according to its classification and regulatory requirements (e.g. in terms of anonymization, encryption, and access controls).
- When it applies, ensure that procedures to ask the user for consent before collecting, storing, or processing personal data are in place.
